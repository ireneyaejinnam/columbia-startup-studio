# Meta Blueprint: Fundamentals of Digital Marketing — Progress Report

## Overview

For the class requirement to complete **Meta Blueprint: Fundamentals of Digital Marketing**, we engaged fully with all available **free learning resources and practice tests** provided through the Meta Blueprint platform.

---

## What I Completed

### Free Learning Resources
- Studied all free Meta Blueprint course materials covering:
  - The value of Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger for businesses
  - Establishing a business presence on Meta platforms
  - Advertising fundamentals (campaign objectives, ad formats, audiences, budgets)
  - Ads Manager structure and campaign creation
  - Reporting and performance metrics

### Practice Tests
We completed and passed **all available practice tests** for the Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate exam, each consisting of 30 questions covering:

- Campaign objectives (Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, Sales, App Promotion)
- Ad formats (Carousel, Collection, Stories, Slideshow, Video, Single Image)
- Audience types (Saved, Custom, Lookalike)
- Budget types (Daily, Lifetime, Advantage Campaign Budget)
- Ads Manager structure (Campaign → Ad Set → Ad levels)
- Key metrics (Impressions, Reach, Frequency, Conversions)
- Meta tools (Meta Pixel, Conversions API, Meta Business Suite)
- Advertising policies and data privacy (COPPA, GDPR, CCPA)

---

## Why We Did Not Complete the Paid Certification Exam

While we completed all **free** components of the Meta Blueprint program, we were unable to register for and complete the official proctored certification exam for the following reasons:

### Cost
The **Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate exam costs $99**. This is a significant out-of-pocket expense that was not communicated as a course requirement ahead of time, and presented a financial barrier.

### Registration Process
The exam registration process — including setting up a proctored online testing environment, installing required software, and arranging a suitable testing space — was logistically burdensome beyond what was anticipated for a class requirement.

---

## Summary

We have demonstrated thorough knowledge of the Meta Blueprint: Fundamentals of Digital Marketing curriculum through completion of all free course materials and passing all available practice exams. The only outstanding component is the paid, proctored certification exam, which was not completed due to its cost and registration requirements.
