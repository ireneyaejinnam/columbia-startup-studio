# Weekly Update - April 7, 2026

## What We Built Last Week

We improved the wardrobe creation flow after peer review feedback showed that users understood the fashion/wardrobe concept but were confused by the first steps. The biggest product learning was that vision-based wardrobe creation feels like the most differentiated part of misfitAI, so we started shifting the experience toward clearer onboarding and fewer overlapping entry paths.

We also tightened the clothing search flow so search results are more fashion-specific. This matters because irrelevant search results make the app feel less trustworthy before users reach the outfit recommendation moment.

## What We're Building This Week

This week we are focusing on onboarding and activation. The goal is to help a new user understand the product quickly, create enough wardrobe context, and reach a useful outfit recommendation without needing the team to explain the flow.

We are also continuing analytics work so we can see where users drop off: landing page, signup, wardrobe setup, or outfit generation.

## Biggest Blocker

The biggest blocker is onboarding clarity. If users experience the app as manual data entry before seeing the recommendation value, they may leave before the product has a chance to work.
