# Demand Test Plan

**Product:** misfitAI / Wardrobe Planner  
**Landing page:** https://mkhetrap.github.io/misfitai-landing/

## Audience

The demand test will focus on Columbia University students and early-career professionals in New York who have busy calendars, variable daily contexts, and enough wardrobe friction to care about outfit planning.

Primary audience:

- Ages 20-30.
- Students, interns, and early-career knowledge workers.
- Use Google Calendar or a similar calendar to manage classes, meetings, internships, interviews, and social events.
- Care about looking put together but do not want to spend time planning outfits.

## Message Angles

### Angle A: Calendar Chaos

**Core message:** "One outfit for class, internship, dinner, and the weather."

This angle tests whether users respond to the practical pain of needing one outfit that works across a complicated day.

Example ad copy:

> Class at 10, internship at 1, dinner at 6, rain by 2. misfitAI plans an outfit from your closet that works for the whole day.

### Angle B: Decision Fatigue

**Core message:** "Stop wasting time deciding what to wear."

This angle tests whether users respond more strongly to the emotional and time-cost pain of daily outfit decisions.

Example ad copy:

> You wear the same 3 outfits because deciding takes too long. misfitAI turns your closet and calendar into a ready-to-wear outfit plan.

## Budget

Total budget: approximately **$200**.

Planned allocation:

- $40/day for 5 days.
- Split evenly between Angle A and Angle B at launch.
- Reallocate after 48 hours toward the angle with stronger click-through and waitlist conversion.

## Channels

- Instagram Stories/Reels targeted to Columbia and NYC student audiences.
- LinkedIn posts and DMs from team members for early-career professionals.
- Columbia group chats and student communities for organic distribution.
- Follow-ups with interview participants who showed interest in wardrobe planning or digital closet features.

## Success Threshold

We will consider the test successful if it reaches either:

- **25 waitlist signups** within 5 days, or
- **8% landing-page visitor-to-email conversion** from tracked campaign traffic.

We will not reinterpret results after launch without comparing against this threshold.

## Tracking Plan

All ad and outreach links should include UTMs:

- `utm_source`: instagram, linkedin, whatsapp, groupchat, interview_followup
- `utm_medium`: paid_social, organic_social, dm, referral
- `utm_campaign`: `MisfitAI_WardrobePlanner_20260226`
- `utm_content`: `calendar_chaos` or `decision_fatigue`

Example:

`https://mkhetrap.github.io/misfitai-landing/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=MisfitAI_WardrobePlanner_20260226&utm_content=calendar_chaos`

## Campaign Naming Convention

`MisfitAI_WardrobePlanner_[Angle]_[YYYYMMDD]`

Examples:

- `MisfitAI_WardrobePlanner_CalendarChaos_20260226`
- `MisfitAI_WardrobePlanner_DecisionFatigue_20260226`
