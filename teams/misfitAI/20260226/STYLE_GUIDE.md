# STYLE GUIDE

**Product:** misfitAI / Wardrobe Planner  
**Date:** February 26, 2026

## Visual Tone

Clean, calm, practical, and lightly editorial: a wardrobe assistant that feels useful and polished without becoming high-fashion or overly technical.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary | Deep Olive | `#3F4A32` | CTA buttons, active nav states, emphasis |
| Secondary | Soft Sage | `#DCE6C7` | Hero and section backgrounds, chips |
| Accent | Muted Olive | `#7A8B5E` | Section labels, highlights, supporting headings |
| Background | Warm White | `#FAFAF7` | Page background and content sections |
| Surface | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, recommendation panels, form surfaces |
| Text | Black | `#050505` | Primary headings and body copy |
| Supporting Text | Charcoal Olive | `#2F3528` | Secondary copy and metadata |
| Divider | Light Gray | `#E8E8E2` | Section dividers and subtle borders |

## Component Patterns

### Buttons

- Large rounded pill shape.
- Primary fill: `#3F4A32`.
- Text: white.
- Padding: generous horizontal padding so CTAs feel easy to tap.
- Use for main actions only, especially "Join Waitlist."

### CTA Pattern

- Primary CTA should be short and direct: "Join Waitlist" or "Join the Waitlist."
- CTA appears in the nav and hero.
- CTA should lead to email capture, not a vague learn-more action.

### Cards

- White background.
- Rounded corners, large enough to feel like a product surface.
- Minimal border or soft shadow.
- Used for calendar/outfit recommendation examples and sign-up modules.

### Pills and Tags

- Soft sage background: `#DCE6C7`.
- Deep olive or charcoal text.
- Used for calendar events, weather, and contextual inputs.

### Imagery and Illustration

- Use real clothing product images when showing outfit recommendations.
- Use light line-art wardrobe patterns as background texture.
- Avoid fashion-editorial photography that makes the product feel like a makeover app.

## Brand Language

Use:

- "your closet and calendar"
- "one less decision"
- "plans the day's outfit for you"
- "outfits personalized to your real day"
- "Join Waitlist"

Avoid:

- "AI stylist that transforms your look"
- "endless outfit inspiration"
- "we decide for you"
- "fashion makeover"
