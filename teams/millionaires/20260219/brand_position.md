# Brand Position — QuestCity

**Team Name:** Team Millionaires (DG-ST-AB)
**Date:** February 19, 2026

*Internal document. Canonical language for all surfaces: landing page, copy, features, pitch.*

---

## Brand Thesis

Your city is an adventure game. Most people just don't know how to play it.

---

## Core Product Definition

> **QuestCity helps city-dwellers discover their own neighborhood by turning local exploration into a game with quests, check-ins, and achievements.**

---

## Existence Statement

> **QuestCity exists to make going outside feel like an adventure worth taking.**

---

## Target Identity

> **QuestCity is for someone who has lived in their city for years but feels like they've only scratched the surface — and feels a quiet guilt about it.**

---

## Canonical Language

- **"Your neighborhood is a game board"**
- **"Turn your weekend into a quest"**
- **"Discover what's been there all along"**
- **"Check in. Level up. Go further."**
- **"Adventure mode: on"**

---

## Language to Avoid

- **Avoid:** "hidden gems" — **Because:** overused, feels like travel content marketing
- **Avoid:** "explore your city" — **Because:** generic; every maps/discovery app says this
- **Avoid:** "recommendations" — **Because:** positions us as another list app, not a game
- **Avoid:** "guide" or "itinerary" — **Because:** sounds like travel planning, not play
- **Avoid:** "social network" — **Because:** we're not building a feed; we're building a game

---

## Tone Guidelines

- **Playful, not preachy** — We never lecture you about experiencing life. We invite you to play.
- **Specific, not vague** — "Visit 5 indie coffee shops in your neighborhood" beats "explore your area."
- **Earned, not aspirational** — The product delivers a real moment of discovery, not a mood board of what discovery could feel like.
- **Direct, not humble** — State what the product does. Don't over-qualify.

---

## Positioning Statement

> **For city-dwellers who default to the same places every weekend, QuestCity is the exploration game that makes going somewhere new feel like an achievement — because you literally earn one.**

---

## Brand Values

- **Specificity** — Vague suggestions are why people don't go out. We give people a quest with a specific location. This value governs every product decision.
- **Completion** — A quest that gets started but abandoned is a failed product. We optimize for people finishing things, not starting them.

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Our Response |
|-----------|-------------|
| "I already use Google Maps / Yelp for this" | Those tools tell you where things are. We tell you why to go and give you a reason to actually follow through. |
| "This sounds like Pokémon GO" | Same psychological mechanism — different application. Real venues, real experiences, real social currency. |
| "I'll try it once and forget about it" | Streaks, progression, and unlockable quests are designed specifically to create the next reason to return. |
| "What if I run out of quests?" | We ship 10 quests for NYC at launch and add new ones monthly. User-generated quests come in phase 2. |
