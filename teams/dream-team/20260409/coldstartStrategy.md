## **Cold Start Strategy**

### **1\. What is your cold start type?**

Recruitr’s cold start problem is primarily a **Network** cold start with elements of **Group/Invite**. The platform becomes more valuable as more athletes and coaches join and interact. A single athlete on the platform has limited value unless coaches are active, and coaches only benefit when there is enough athlete density.

### **2\. Who is your hard side?**

The hard side is:

* The 10-15 college coaches and recruiting coordinators already connected to Columbia athletics or local NYC soccer networks  
* The small group of serious athletes actively looking for recruiting exposure and willing to fully build out profiles and upload film

These users create the value everyone else sees. If coaches are not active, athletes will not stay. If strong athlete profiles do not exist, coaches have no reason to engage.

### **3\. What are your two strategies?**

**Primary Strategy: Constrained Launch**  
Recruitr will focus on one dense community first instead of launching broadly. The first target network is college soccer players and coaches connected through Columbia athletics and nearby programs.

**Secondary Strategy: Wizard of Oz**  
The team will manually support recruiting interactions early on by helping onboard athletes, curating profiles, and personally connecting coaches with players before the process is fully automated.
