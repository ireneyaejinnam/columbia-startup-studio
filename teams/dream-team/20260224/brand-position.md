# Brand Position

**Team Name:** Recruitr
**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Version:** 2.0 — polished post-synthetic-testing (revised from Feb 19, 2026 draft)

**Purpose:** This is an internal document. It captures our brand's thesis, identity, and canonical language so that everything we build — landing page, copy, features, pitch — speaks with one voice. This is not marketing copy. It is a decision reference.

This version reflects revisions made after five rounds of synthetic copy testing (V1–V5, 60 personas/round, Feb–Mar 2026) and six live user-testing sessions with college athletes and coaches in April 2026.

---

## Brand Thesis

> **Recruiting should reward talent, not connections.**

---

## Core Product Definition

> **Recruitr helps college soccer athletes and coaches find each other by putting profiles, film, and messaging in one place — replacing the scattered mess of emails, spreadsheets, and highlight links.**

"Soccer" is explicit. V5 synthetic testing surfaced "Is this only for soccer?" as a new dealbreaker objection. We are soccer-first by design — multi-sport expansion is a roadmap question, not a current positioning one. Pretending otherwise creates scope mismatch.

---

## Existence Statement

> **Recruitr exists to make sure talented athletes don't get overlooked just because they didn't know the right people or understand the process.**

---

## Target Identity

**Primary:** A transfer portal athlete or a high school senior committed to a college soccer career, who has already exhausted the obvious channels (cold emails, NCSA, highlight links over text) and is still not getting interviews from programs that fit their level.

V5 synthetic testing was definitive: transfer portal athletes are our strongest segment by every metric (83% resonance, 67% conversion, 0% dealbreaker). They have the most urgent timeline, the least existing tooling, and the highest willingness to try something new. **We lead with them.**

**Secondary:** D2/D3/NAIA/JUCO coaches who don't have an in-house recruiting analyst and are running their entire pipeline out of email, Hudl, and a spreadsheet.

> Recruitr is for someone who believes that where you train shouldn't determine whether you get seen — that a midfielder in Denver deserves the same shot as one in a coach's backyard.

## Canonical Language

These are the lines we use. They're either tested winners from synthetic evaluation or canonical phrases pulled directly from user interviews. Use them. Don't reword them.

- **"Stop emailing into the void."** — Canonical headline. Reframes the problem in athlete language. Pulled from Brandon Newman's interview.
- **"Get seen."** — Core athlete promise. Three words. Use as section header or button copy.
- **"One profile. Every coach."** — Build once, be discoverable everywhere.
- **"Real coaches, real interest."** — Trust frame. Verified coaches, transparent engagement.
- **"Recruiting, reimagined."** — Tested winner in V3–V5 (46 mentions as strongest line). Reframe for the product.
- **"The LinkedIn of college sports meets TikTok."** — Strongest tested line across all rounds (67 mentions). Comprehension shortcut.
- **"Video-first profiles coaches can evaluate in seconds."** — Tested winner. Names the differentiating capability.

---

## Language to Avoid

- **Avoid:** "AI-powered" or "smart algorithm" — Overpromises on tech we don't have yet. Sounds like every other startup. Workflow problem, not data-science problem.
- **Avoid:** "Disrupt" or "revolutionize" — Recruiting isn't broken because it lacks innovation. It's broken because it's fragmented. We consolidate, not disrupt.
- **Avoid:** "The future of [X] is here" — Generic AI-startup framing. V1 testing showed personas called this "AI-written" and "something I'd scroll past." Single biggest reason for V1's 38% dealbreaker rate.
- **Avoid:** "Network" or "social platform" — We're not building another feed. Coaches told us: "It can't just be another highlight feed. It has to add structure." (Kevin Wehner)
- **Avoid:** "Exposure" (on its own) — NCSA promises exposure. Our value is what comes after exposure — the message, the reply, the follow-up.
- **Avoid:** "All sports" or "every sport" — Untrue today. Soccer-first by design. Saying otherwise creates scope mismatch flagged in V5 testing.

---

## Tone Guidelines

- **Direct not hype-y** — Say what the product does. Don't oversell. Athletes and coaches are skeptical of platforms that promise everything.
- **Confident not corporate** — Small team that talks to users every week. Write like that, not like a press release.
- **Empathetic not patronizing** — Athletes are stressed. Coaches are overwhelmed. Acknowledge the grind without pretending we magically fix it.
- **Specific not vague** — "250 emails a day" beats "coaches are busy." Numbers and named people beat adjectives.
- **Athlete first, coach second** — When in doubt, write to the athlete. The athlete is the emotional decision and the supply side. Coaches will arrive through workflow value; athletes arrive through hope.

---

## Positioning Statement

> **For college soccer athletes — especially transfers and high school seniors — and the D2/D3/NAIA/JUCO coaches recruiting them, Recruitr is the recruiting platform that turns scattered emails and highlight links into a single searchable profile and direct messaging system, because the current process buries talent under inbox noise and spreadsheet chaos.**

---

## Brand Values

- **Simplicity over features** — If a feature adds complexity without solving a clear pain point from interviews, we cut it. The product should feel lighter than what coaches and athletes use today, not heavier.
- **Transparency over polish** — We'd rather show athletes which coaches viewed their profile than build a prettier highlight player. Real information beats aesthetics.
- **Access over exclusivity** — A D3 athlete in a small town gets the same shot as a showcase kid. Design decisions widen the funnel, not narrow it.
- **Verified over viral** — Growth that compromises trust kills the product. Every coach is manually verified, even if it slows demand-side onboarding.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Our Response |
|-----------|--------------|
| "I already use NCSA / SportsRecruits / Hudl — why switch?" | Those tools do one thing well (database, profiles, or film). None combine search, profiles, film, and messaging in one place. You're still bouncing between tabs and spreadsheets. Recruitr is the single workflow. |
| "How do I know real coaches are on here?" | Every coach is manually verified before they can message athletes. You see their school, division, and team on every message. No anonymous accounts. |
| "Another recruiting platform? What makes this different?" | We're not a highlight feed and we're not a database. We're the place where the actual connection happens — coach finds athlete, coach messages athlete, athlete responds. One workflow, start to finish. |
| "Is this only for soccer?" | Yes, today. Soccer is where the founding team has lived and where we've done our research. We're soccer-first by design. Multi-sport expansion is on the roadmap but is not a 2026 commitment. |
| "How many people are actually on the platform?" | We're early — under 50 accounts as of mid-May 2026. We're not pretending to be NCSA. We're growing through warm intros from college coaches and player-to-player referrals. If that's a dealbreaker, come back in three months. |

## What Changed Since V1 (Feb 19, 2026)

- **Target sharpened to transfer portal first.** V5 synthetic data was definitive: 83% resonance vs. 67% in HS athletes. Lead with the most urgent customer.
- **"LinkedIn meets TikTok" elevated to canonical.** Won every round of testing. Was in copy but not in the brand doc; now anchored so it doesn't drift.
- **"Stop emailing into the void" added as canonical headline.** From Brandon Newman's interview. Reframes the problem in the athlete's own language.
- **Soccer scope made explicit.** V5 surfaced "Is this only for soccer?" as a new dealbreaker. Adding the explicit scope position removes ambiguity.
- **"Future of X" framing explicitly banned.** Single biggest reason V1 scored 38% dealbreakers.
- **Brand value added: "Verified over viral."** Reflects the trade-off we made on coach onboarding speed.
