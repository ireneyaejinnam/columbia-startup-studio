## Section 3 — How It Works

### Recruiting, reimagined.

The LinkedIn of college sports meets TikTok — built so coaches can evaluate film in seconds, not paragraphs.

**For athletes:**

1. **Build your profile.** Bio, stats, school, position, grad year, GPA, and your best highlights. 5 minutes.
2. **Go live.** Toggle your profile to "Active" and show up in coach search results.
3. **Get messaged.** When a verified coach sees something they like, they reach out. You reply when you're interested.

**For coaches:**

1. **Search the pool.** Filter by sport, position, grad year, region, GPA.
2. **Watch the film.** Every profile has highlights ready to evaluate. No 3-tab workflow.
3. **Reach out directly.** Message athletes from inside the platform. Everything in one place.

## Section 5 — Common Questions

**How is this different from NCSA or SportsRecruits?**
Those tools do one thing well — a database, or profiles, or film hosting. None of them combine search, profiles, film, and direct messaging in one place. You're still bouncing between tabs and spreadsheets. Recruitr is the single workflow.

**Is this only for soccer?**
Yes, today. Soccer is where we've done our research and where the founding team has lived the recruiting process. Multi-sport expansion is on our roadmap but not a 2026 commitment.

**How do I know coaches are real?**
Every coach is manually verified before they can message athletes. You see their school, division, and team on every message they send. If we can't verify them, they can't reach out.

**Is it really free?**
Yes — for athletes. Always. We make money from college programs that subscribe for advanced search and shortlist tools. You build your profile and use the platform for free, forever.

**How many people are on it?**
We're early. Under 50 athletes and a handful of verified coaches as of mid-May 2026. We're growing through warm intros from college coaches and player-to-player referrals — not paid ads. If you want a platform that's already huge, NCSA exists. If you want a platform built for how recruiting actually works, you're in the right place.
