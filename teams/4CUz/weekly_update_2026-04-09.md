# **Weekly Update — April 9**

## **What we built last week and why**

Last week, we focused on making Tripable feel like a real collaborative trip-planning workspace instead of just separate planning pages. We added more itinerary functionality, including day creation, drag-and-drop, map marker fixes, and improved activity/list behavior. We also worked on sharing and profile-related flows, including the share popup, rename/edit buttons, profile photo updates, pending invitations, and notifications. This mattered because our core user flow depends on users being able to create a trip, organize plans, and bring other people into the workspace without getting stuck.

We also added commenting functionality and more granular comments inside sections, plus availability hover behavior so users can see who is available on a specific date. These were deliberate choices because group planning is not just about storing information; users need lightweight ways to react, discuss, and understand what the group is doing. GitHub shows work around comments, hover availability, pending invitations, profile updates, and itinerary improvements during this period.

## **What we’re building this week and why**

This week, we are tightening the onboarding and trip flow. The biggest priority is making sure users understand what to do after entering a trip: add plans, check availability, invite friends, or organize expenses. We are also continuing to fix bugs that came up while testing the collaboration flow.

## **Biggest blocker**

Our biggest blocker is still onboarding clarity. The product has the main pieces, but a new user may not immediately know which action to take first after signing in or opening a trip.
