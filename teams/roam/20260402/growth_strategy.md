# Roam — Growth Strategy

**Team:** Roam  
**Live for ios in TestFlight:** https://testflight.apple.com/join/CHpUX4F4

## Product Overview

Roam helps users turn social discovery into action by organizing things they want to do and making it easier to follow through. For this sprint, our growth work focused on onboarding and early repeat usage.

## Target Audience

College students in NYC, starting with Columbia and nearby student communities where social coordination is frequent and density is high.

---

## Part 1 — The Plan (Submitted Apr 2)

### Channel 1: Direct Community Seeding (Primary)

**Approach:** Introduce Roam in person to students at parties, events, and campus social settings. Ask users to sign up immediately and complete one core action during the first session.

**Volume commitment:** Team-run direct outreach across 5 days with in-person asks at events.

**What success looks like:**
- Signups attributable to direct outreach
- Higher completion through onboarding
- Users returning after initial testing

### Channel 2: Cold Text / DM

**Approach:** Personalized outreach to warm and semi-cold contacts via iMessage, WhatsApp, and Instagram DM with a clear ask: 5-minute test and honest feedback.

**Volume commitment:** 30 outbound messages over 5 days, with follow-ups to non-converters.

**Draft message (cold-ish):**  
"hey - quick one. we're building Roam and testing with real users this week. it takes ~5 min and we'd love blunt feedback on what's confusing. cool if i send the link?"

**Draft message (warm):**  
"need a favor - can you try Roam and tell me exactly where it breaks/confuses you? we need real usage data this week. [link]"

**What success looks like:**
- Reply/open rate
- Signup conversion from sent messages
- Return usage after first session

### Overall Definition of Success

This strategy works if we convert real users (not just clicks), get them through onboarding, and see users come back on their own after first use.

---

## Part 2 — Results (Apr 9 update)

### Channel Results

- **Direct approach produced signups.** In-person and high-trust outreach was the strongest channel.
- **Cold text/DM helped top-of-funnel volume**, but direct community introductions converted better.

### Funnel Snapshot (from analytics)

- **Total users:** 32  
- **New users:** 29  
- **Sessions:** 41  
- **Views per session:** 2.07  
- **Avg session duration:** 31s

### PMF / Return Signal

We define `active_users` as users who came back on their own after testing.

- **30-day user activity:** 34  
- **7-day user activity (consistent returners):** 2  
- **1-day user activity:** 0

### What Worked

- Direct community seeding in dense student environments.
- Context-rich outreach ("use this now") instead of passive posting.

### What Did Not Work

- Passive exposure without direct context had weaker conversion.
- We still have a drop between first session and weekly return usage.

### What We Are Changing Next

1. Double down on direct campus/event seeding.
2. Tighten onboarding so value is clear in the first session.
3. Add stronger return triggers to increase weekly active returners.
