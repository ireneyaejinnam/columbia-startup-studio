# Roam — User Testing Synthesis

**Team:** Roam  
**Sessions run:** 3-5 onboarding tests with student users and friend-of-friend participants.

## What We Observed

The strongest cross-session pattern was that users understood Roam faster when onboarding happened with direct social context (in-person intro, party/event conversation), but many dropped after initial testing unless the next action was very clear.

In short: acquisition signal was good, but repeat behavior depended heavily on first-session clarity.

## What We Are Changing

1. Make first-session onboarding more explicit, with a clearer "what to do next" action.
2. Reduce friction points that cause early bounce during the first flow.
3. Add stronger return prompts so users come back on their own after initial testing.

## Why

User testing showed that confusion is less about interest in the concept and more about follow-through after first use. This matches our analytics signal: direct outreach generated signups, but returning users are still a smaller subset. The product decision is therefore to prioritize onboarding clarity and return behavior, not just top-of-funnel volume.
