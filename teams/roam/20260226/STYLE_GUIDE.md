## Visual Tone

Energetic but calm — like a friend who always knows what to do and never makes it a big deal.

## Component Patterns

- **Button:** Rounded pill, primary orange fill, white text, 16px
- **CTA:** Full-width on mobile, centered on desktop — action language ("Go Now", "Find Something")
- **Card:** 12px rounded corners, soft drop shadow, image on top / text below
