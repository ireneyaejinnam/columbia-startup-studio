# Weekly Update — August Fan Club (Evidence of Life)
**Week of April 20, 2026**

## What we built last week and why

Shipped the redesigned "add places" flow — users can now search for a location, tag it with a category (Food, Coffee, Outdoors, Culture), and save it in one tap. We prioritized this because it was the #1 usability blocker and the map + heatmap view is one of the strongest moments in the product. Concierge users are now actually adding places instead of abandoning the feature.

We also landed on a tighter product framing that we think works better than "private timeline." The pitch is evolving toward Evidence of Life as a **personal life OS** — one timeline with three layers: Plan (what I meant to do), Reflect (what I actually did), and Evidence (what I can understand and carry forward). This came out of user interviews — people didn't just want a record of the past, they wanted something that connects planning, tracking, and reflection in one place. That's the gap none of the alternatives fill.

Competitive positioning copy is live on the landing page. We're benchmarking against Apple Notes, Notion, Instagram, and paper journals — showing that EoL is the only tool that combines moments, AI tags, map, calendar, and reflection.

## What we're building this week and why

Feature freeze starts now. No new features — stability, polish, and bug fixes only. We're compiling usage data, collecting testimonials from the concierge cohort, and building the Demo Day pitch narrative. Shan is leading the deck. Iris is running exit interviews to get 2–3 usable soundbites. Judy is finalizing brand assets.

## Current user count

Registered users: ~24. User interviews conducted to date: ~40. We're nearing the 50-interview mark across three months of research. Engagement with the Places/map feature specifically has been the strongest signal — it's what makes people say "oh, cool."

## Biggest blocker

Narrative clarity. The "personal life OS" framing is stronger than "private timeline," but we need to commit and build the entire Demo Day story around it. The three-layer framework (Plan / Reflect / Evidence) needs to be airtight by next week.
