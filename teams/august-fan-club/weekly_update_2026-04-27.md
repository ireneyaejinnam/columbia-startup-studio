# Weekly Update — August Fan Club (Evidence of Life)
**Week of April 27, 2026**

## What we built last week and why

We broke feature freeze for one thing: an **AI Wrapped feature** — a generated recap of your week that summarizes where you went, what you focused on, and highlights worth remembering. We built this because the Wrapped concept was the most-requested idea from user feedback across three months of interviews, and it's the clearest demonstration of the "Evidence" layer in our three-part framework (Plan / Reflect / Evidence). It ties together everything else in the product — your tasks, your places, your timeline — into a single shareable moment. This is also the feature we think has the best shot at driving organic sharing and word-of-mouth growth.

Beyond that, we held the freeze. Fixed a timezone rendering issue where the "time remaining" display was off for users who hadn't set their schedule cycle. Fixed a localization gap where some UI labels stayed in English in Chinese mode. Small but visible polish items that would undermine a live demo.
