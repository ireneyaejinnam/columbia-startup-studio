# Product Brief Template
**Team Name:** August Fan Club
**Date:** February 24, 2026

---

## The Problem
What problem are you solving? Describe it in the language your interviewees used, not startup jargon.

- People go through their days without a clear sense of what they accomplished. They look back and feel like they wasted their time because their to-do list wasn't fulfilled. Over days, weeks, and months, they forget what actually happened and lose track of meaningful moments.
- Some people use Instagram Stories to capture images as a recap of their day. Others rely on to-do list apps, but none of these tools offer a "done list" — a way to reflect on what was actually completed.
- Several interviewees expressed interest in trying a solution like this.

---

## The Solution
What are you building? Describe the core product in 2-3 sentences.

- We are building a platform that integrates a to-do list with a done list. As users complete tasks, those items are automatically added to a calendar view. Users can also manually log events, activities, and places to the calendar. At any point, they can look back at a given day, week, or month and see everything that happened over that time frame.
- Some people currently use physical notebooks for this kind of tracking, but our product digitalizes and streamlines the process.

---

## Target User
Who specifically is this for? Be as concrete as your interview data allows.

- Our interviews focused on students, but the product is relevant to anyone across all walks of life.
- The ideal user is someone whose days involve varied tasks and activities — anyone who wants a better way to track what they've done and reflect on how they spend their time.

---

## Why Now

No comparable product currently exists in the market that combines a to-do list, done list, and calendar-based reflection tool in one place.

---

## Key Interview Evidence
Summarize the strongest signals from your interviews:

- **Strongest quote:** Interviewees used phrases like "easy to use with less effort," "reminder and look back," "helpful," "less stressful," and "organize my thoughts."
- **Pattern:** Users prefer something with semi-automatic tracking to make logging as effortless as possible.
- **Surprise:** Everyone has a different process for managing their to-do list and recording what they've done — there is no single dominant tool or workflow.
- **Current spend/effort:** Most interviewees are not currently paying for a tool like this.

---

## Open Questions
What don't you know yet? What assumptions still need testing?

1. What kind of UI/UX do users prefer? This could be explored through A/B testing different layouts and workflows.
2. Would enough people pay for this product to make it financially sustainable?
3. What additional features do users want? Many interviewees said they would pay for a product with more functionality — but are these desired features combinable into a single product, or does everyone want something different?
