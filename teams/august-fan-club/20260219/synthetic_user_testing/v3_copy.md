How It Works
One timeline. Three layers.
Plan
Your existing calendar events appear automatically.
Not as commitments.
As intentions.
Actual
Your day is reconstructed from signals you already generate — completed events, time blocks, activity patterns.
You can adjust or add something in seconds.
But you never start from a blank page.
Plans change. Reality matters more.
Memory
Photos, places, and moments connect to each day.
So your timeline isn't just tasks.
It becomes context. Emotion. Proof.
