How It Works
One timeline. Three layers.
Plan
Your existing calendar events appear automatically.
Not as commitments.
As intentions.
Actual
Your day is reconstructed from signals you already generate — completed events, time blocks, activity patterns.
You can adjust or add something in seconds.
But you never start from a blank page.
Plans change. Reality matters more.
Memory
Photos, places, and moments connect to each day.
So your timeline isn't just tasks.
It becomes context. Emotion. Proof.

The Weekly Recap
Open it once a week. Get your life back.
Every Sunday, Evidence of Life builds a recap of your week — automatically. No setup. No writing. No habit to maintain.
Here's what one looks like:
YOUR WEEK · FEB 24 – MAR 1

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DID
  Worked 38h (planned 45) · 4 deep-work sessions
  Saw 6 different people · 2 long walks · 1 unplanned trip

WHAT YOU PLANNED BUT DIDN'T
  Dentist (rescheduled to Mar 10)
  3 gym sessions (did 1)
  "Read for 30 min" — every day

MOMENTS WORTH KEEPING
  Tuesday sunset walk · Maya's birthday dinner
  Saturday morning at the farmer's market

WHERE YOUR TIME WENT
  Work 38h · Friends 9h · Solo 14h · Errands 6h
  Lost time (unaccounted): 11h
A five-minute scroll, and you understand a week you barely remember living.
